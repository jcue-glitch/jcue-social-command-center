## Starter Themes To Look For

- Overstimulation and digital minimalism.
- Slow living and ordinary rituals.
- Modern masculinity without alpha/hustle framing.
- Taste, style, fashion, and object culture.
- Creative life, quiet ambition, and building in public without over-announcing.
- Tokyo/Japan as atmosphere, not travel content.
