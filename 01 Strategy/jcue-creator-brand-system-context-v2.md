# JCue Creator + Brand System Context v2

Date: 2026-05-12

This document adds to the existing JCue creator strategy. It does not replace the previous wabi-sabi, 80s Japan vintage, calm lifestyle, beauty/skincare, and future dry flower/stone arrangement direction.

## Core Identity

Jimmy Cue is a Tokyo/Japan-based creative strategist, content creator, and visual director blending mindful living, cinematic lifestyle, fashion/editorial culture, cafe culture, vintage/analog aesthetics, modern masculinity, premium visual storytelling, and platform-native short-form video.

The content should feel real, reflective, emotionally grounded, stylish without trying too hard, aspirational but attainable, culturally aware, and quietly confident.

The goal is not "influencer."

The goal is: interesting person with taste, perspective, and atmosphere.

## Positioning

Jimmy Cue content is mindful masculine slow living through a cinematic Tokyo lifestyle lens.

The audience should feel: I want my life to feel like this.

## Overall Feel

The content should feel like:

- A cool person's real life.
- Thoughtful but not preachy.
- Emotionally intelligent but casual.
- Stylish but grounded.
- Masculine but soft around the edges.
- Calm in an overstimulated world.

Think slow living, digital minimalism, quiet luxury, wabi-sabi, reflective masculinity, thoughtful routines, Tokyo side streets, old cafes, and tactile everyday moments.

## Video Structure

The first 1-2 seconds are everything.

Hooks should stop scrolling immediately, trigger emotional recognition, feel personal, and feel culturally relevant.

Good hook patterns:

- "i think we're all a little too overstimulated lately"
- "normalize disappearing for a while"
- "mindful living changed me more than success advice ever did"
- "the older i get, the less i care about looking weird"

Editing style:

- Fast but calm pacing.
- 0.7-1.5 second clips.
- Minimal transitions.
- Natural sound moments.
- Soft music.
- Layered atmosphere.

Avoid overediting, meme pacing, loud sound effects, and trend spam.

## Current Copy Style Overlay

Use `jcue-copy-style-system-v1.md` as the active short-form copy generator style. This overlay sharpens on-screen hooks, captions, and hashtags toward direct, assertive, philosophical, concise, soft masculine lifestyle copy.

The copy should feel like calm authority from someone who built a life intentionally. It should not sound like influencer motivation, vague healing language, hustle culture, poetic aesthetic writing, or generic self-help.

Use strong negative or contrarian hooks such as:

- "most people..."
- "your ___ is not the problem"
- "you are not lazy, you are ___"
- "modern life is destroying ___"
- "the way you ___ shapes the way you ___"
- "people underestimate ___"
- "___ is not aesthetic. it is ___"

Caption rhythm:

1. Strong thesis.
2. Contrarian clarification.
3. Explanation of why it matters.
4. Tie back to Jimmy's lifestyle.
5. Final memorable line.
6. Four to six hashtags, always including #jimmycue.
