# JCue Content Copy Style System v1

Date: 2026-05-19

This document defines Jimmy Cue's preferred short-form copy style for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. It should guide on-screen hooks, captions, and hashtags inside the daily content brief.

## Goal

Create stronger short-form copy that feels like calm authority from someone who built a life intentionally.

The copy should be assertive, philosophical, concise, soft masculine, lifestyle-focused, grounded in creator search insights, and practical enough to film inside Jimmy's real apartment, commute, cafe, gym, style, coffee, music, and creative-life moments.

Use Dan Koe-style structure as inspiration only: clear thesis, contrarian framing, principle-based explanation, and segmented essay rhythm. Do not imitate another creator's exact voice.

## Tone

Use:

- Direct statements.
- Clean pacing.
- Principle-based thinking.
- Minimal emotional explanation.
- Observational confidence.
- Philosophical but practical framing.
- Identity, routine, environment, and self-perception language.

Avoid:

- Influencer motivational captions.
- Vague healing language.
- Hustle culture.
- Over-soft journaling.
- Poetic aesthetic writing.
- Generic wellness advice.
- Generic self-help.
- Too much vulnerability.
- Weak positioning.

## On-Screen Hook Formula

Use a strong negative or contrarian hook that can be read instantly.

Good patterns:

- "most people..."
- "your ___ is not the problem"
- "you are not lazy, you are ___"
- "modern life is destroying ___"
- "the way you ___ shapes the way you ___"
- "people underestimate ___"
- "___ is not aesthetic. it is ___"

Example hooks:

- "your nervous system was not designed for modern life"
- "most people are addicted to rushing through their own life"
- "the way you dress shapes the way you think"
- "most people are overstimulated, not lazy"
- "your daily rhythm becomes your psychology"
- "style is not surface level"
- "slow living is not doing less"

## Caption Formula

Captions should read like short essays with strong segmentation:

1. Strong thesis.
2. Contrarian clarification.
3. Explanation of why it matters.
4. Tie back to Jimmy's lifestyle.
5. Final memorable line.
6. Hashtags.

Use short paragraphs. Avoid long lists unless the content concept specifically calls for a list.

## Preferred Phrases

- "Most people underestimate..."
- "That is why I take ___ seriously."
- "This is not about ___."
- "It is about ___."
- "Your environment shapes your psychology."
- "Your routine becomes your identity."
- "The object is not the point."
- "The system around it is."
- "That changes everything over time."

## Avoided Phrases

- "I used to think..."
- "lately I've been realizing..."
- "maybe..."
- "trying to..."
- "healing"
- "romanticizing"
- "self care" as a generic phrase

## Example

On-screen hook:

the way you dress shapes the way you think

Caption:

Most people treat clothing as surface level.

It is not.

What you wear influences your posture, your energy, your self-perception, the way you move through the day, and the standards you unconsciously hold yourself to.

That is why I care about fit, fabric, silhouette, and details.

Not to impress strangers.

To create alignment between how I see myself internally and how I present myself externally.

Style becomes different when you stop chasing trends and start building identity.

The outfit is not the point.

The person you become while wearing it is.
