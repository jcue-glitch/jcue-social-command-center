# JCue Creator Strategy v1

Date: 2026-04-30

## Core Idea

Design Stillness. Live Artfully.

Jimmy's content should feel like a calm, stylish friend sharing daily life without pretending everything is finished. The audience comes for aesthetic taste, but stays for warmth, reflection, and the feeling that imperfect beauty is livable.

## Content Pillars

- Wabi-sabi daily life: apartment, commute, texture, imperfection, quiet noticing.
- 80s Japan vintage style: outfits, fabric, silhouette, old Tokyo mood, worn-in detail.
- GRWM as friendship: talking while getting ready, simple routines, small confidence.
- Material studies: dry flowers, stone, fabric, vessels, shadows, and future luxury-space direction.
- Creative direction diary: what Jimmy is learning about taste, calmness, and building a creative life.

## Platform Roles

- TikTok: hook testing, personality, trends, daily intimacy.
- Instagram Reels: shareable friendship and visual polish.
- YouTube Shorts: searchable evergreen philosophy and routine content.
- Instagram photo/carousel: saveable aesthetic authority.
- Stories: polls, behind-the-scenes, small daily conversation.
- YouTube long-form: biweekly deeper connection and creator-world building.

## Havenotats Reference

Use Havenotats as a reference for casual connection, weekly life pacing, warm B-roll, and the feeling of spending time with the creator.

Do not copy his home design, camera, gear, or cafe-guide topics. Jimmy's long-form lane is life, thoughts, creative direction, style, beauty, wabi-sabi, and building toward dry flower and stone arrangements.

## Algorithm Working Rules

- TikTok: optimize the first second, 3-5 second bridge, searchable spoken words, captions, and specific hashtags.
- Reels: optimize watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach; create lines people would DM to a friend.
- Shorts: use direct searchable titles and satisfy the promise quickly.
- Photo/carousels: create tactile close-ups and short philosophical lines that make viewers swipe, save, or comment.

## Morning Filming Standard

The daily pack must fit apartment or commute capture in 30-45 minutes. The first frame should already contain the tension: beautiful but imperfect, calm but real, stylish but approachable.
