Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

Purpose

JCue Social Command Center is a private creator dashboard used to organize content planning, research, and authorized social performance data for Jimmy Cue.

Data Collected

When the account owner connects a social platform, the dashboard may process permitted profile information, video metadata, post URLs, timestamps, and performance metrics such as views, likes, comments, shares, saves, watch time, or similar analytics where the platform allows access.

How Data Is Used

Data is used to understand content performance, identify patterns, create planning notes, and improve future content briefs. The dashboard does not sell personal data.

Storage

Private OAuth credentials and access tokens are intended to remain on the account owner's local device or secure private infrastructure. Public static dashboard files should not contain secrets, passwords, access tokens, or refresh tokens.

Sharing

Performance data may be written to the account owner's private Notion workspace or other owner-controlled storage when explicitly configured. Data is not intentionally shared with unrelated third parties.

Revoking Access

The account owner may revoke connected platform access from the relevant platform's app/account settings at any time.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the site owner through the associated creator/business channels.
